# Supply Chain Performance Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes supply chain performance to provide data-driven insights into sales, customers, products, profitability, and logistics operations.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics process using Microsoft Excel, MySQL, and Power BI.

## 2. Business Problem

The company needs a clear, data-driven view of its sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and delivery operations.

The analysis helps management identify performance trends, profitable products and markets, customer patterns, and logistics issues that may require attention.

## 3. Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and preparation
- MySQL – Data analysis and business queries
- Power BI – Interactive dashboards and data visualization

## 4. Project Process

1. Data understanding
2. Data cleaning and preparation
3. Database and table creation
4. SQL business analysis
5. KPI development
6. Power BI data modeling
7. Dashboard development
8. Business insights and recommendations

## 5. Dashboards

The project contains four Power BI dashboards:

### Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of overall business performance, including sales, profit, orders, and key performance trends.

### Sales & Customer Performance
Analyzes sales trends, customer segments, markets, and customer performance.

### Product Management Performance
Analyzes product sales, quantity sold, profitability, categories, and product performance.

### Supply Chain & Logistics Performance
Analyzes shipping modes, delivery performance, late deliveries, markets, and logistics efficiency.

## 6. SQL Analysis

SQL was used to answer business questions related to:

- Overall business performance
- Sales trends
- Customer performance
- Product performance
- Profitability
- Market performance
- Shipping and delivery performance

## 7. Key Business Questions

The analysis addresses questions such as:

- What are the company's total sales, profit, and orders?
- How has sales revenue changed over time?
- Which markets generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
- Which products and categories perform best?
- Which shipping modes have the highest late-delivery rates?
- Which markets experience the most delivery delays?
- How does actual delivery time compare with scheduled delivery time?

## 8. Expected Business Value

The analysis provides management with insights that can support:

- Revenue growth
- Profitability improvement
- Customer management
- Product decisions
- Market performance evaluation
- Shipping and logistics improvement
- Data-driven decision making
